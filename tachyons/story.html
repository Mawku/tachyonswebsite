01 / The name

The Origin of Tachyons

In physics, a tachyon is a hypothetical particle that always moves faster than light. It challenges the standard understanding of causality and time.

Or at least, this is what I told my teammates back in early 2026 when we created our RoboCup squad.

02 / The beginning

It started as
a robotics squad.

Yes, you read that right. It all started as a robotics squad that reached the World Cup in Incheon, South Korea.

The squad kind of got torn apart. And there I was, the one behind the name and the logo, deciding that it couldn't end like this. That's why I chose to use it for my startup.

— Angelo Caruso, founder of Tachyons
