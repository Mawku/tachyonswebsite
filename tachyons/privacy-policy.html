This notice explains how the Tachyons website handles information when you browse its pages or send a message through the contact form.

Data controller and contact

The data controller is Angelo Caruso, operating this website under the name Tachyons in Italy. You can contact the controller at contact.tachyons@gmail.com about this notice or to exercise your data protection rights.

This notice covers the Tachyons website at tachyons.eu and its preview pages. If a private preview requires a platform account, that platform's separate account and sign-in processing is covered by its own privacy notice, including OpenAI’s privacy notice for ChatGPT-hosted previews.

Information we process

  • Contact requests: your email address, subject, message, and any other information you choose to include. Please do not send sensitive information or information about other people that is unnecessary for your request.
  • Technical connection data: delivery and security providers process data such as an IP address, request time, requested resource, and browser or network information to serve the website and protect it against abuse.

The contact fields are needed to send and answer a message. Providing them is voluntary; without an email address and message, we cannot respond through the form. You can browse the public pages without creating a Tachyons account.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Responding to general enquiries: our legitimate interest in receiving and answering messages addressed to us (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Steps you request before a possible agreement: where applicable, Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.
  • Website delivery, security, and aggregate operational statistics: our legitimate interests in operating a reliable website, preventing abuse, and understanding overall traffic (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR), subject to necessity and the interests and rights of visitors.
  • Legal obligations: Article 6(1)(c) GDPR, where a specific obligation applies.

Contact details are used to deal with your request. Submitting a message does not subscribe you to marketing emails or SMS. We do not use the website to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on visitors.

Hosting and traffic statistics

Cloudflare provides delivery and security services for the public domain. It processes connection information, including IP addresses, and can provide aggregated reports such as request counts by country.

The website's own code does not load a visitor analytics beacon or maintain a separate database of visitor IP addresses. Any use of hosting traffic reports is limited to aggregate counts and country-level trends for this website, rather than identifying individual visitors or following them across websites.

A country inferred from an IP address is approximate. Request counts can include automated traffic and multiple requests for one page; they are not an exact count of people.

See Cloudflare's privacy notice for its handling of technical and network data.

Cookies and local features

The website's own code does not set advertising, profiling, or analytics cookies. Cloudflare may use necessary security cookies, such as cf_clearance, to verify requests and protect the website against automated abuse. Their lifetime depends on the security configuration and verification state; details are available in Cloudflare's cookie documentation.

The page banner is a link to this notice. Closing it is not consent to tracking. Website search runs in your browser without sending your search terms to an external search service. Fonts and visual media are served with the website rather than requested from an external font or video platform.

You can manage cookies through your browser settings. Restricting necessary security cookies may affect access to protected pages.

Contact form and service providers

The contact form sends the details you enter to Formspree when you submit it. Formspree handles the submission and email delivery; it may also process technical information, including your IP address, for delivery, spam prevention, and security. We receive and answer correspondence using email services.

Access to message content is limited to the people and service providers needed to handle the request, and to authorities where disclosure is required by law. The form does not load a Formspree tracking script when you simply browse the page.

Formspree states that its infrastructure includes the United States. Hosting and email providers may also process information outside the European Economic Area. International processing locations and applicable protections must be considered separately from the absence of analytics cookies. For Formspree's practices, see its privacy notice. You can contact us for information about the providers and transfer arrangements applicable to your request.

Links to services such as Discord open their own websites. Those services apply their own privacy notices when you visit or use them.

Retention and security

Contact messages are retained for the time needed to answer and resolve the request. Once the correspondence is no longer needed, it is deleted unless a specific ongoing matter, legal obligation, or legal claim requires limited further retention.

Technical data held by delivery and security providers follows the periods and settings applicable to their services. We do not copy technical visitor IP records into a separate statistics database. Information about applicable retention settings can be requested using the contact address above.

Connections to the website and contact endpoint use HTTPS. Access to correspondence must be restricted to authorised people. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Your rights

Subject to the conditions in the GDPR, you can request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing, and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. If processing is based on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing.

Send a request to contact.tachyons@gmail.com. Requests are normally answered within one month; a permitted extension will be explained within that month. We may request only the information necessary to verify the requester's identity.

You can also complain to the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante) or another competent supervisory authority.

Changes to this notice

This notice will be updated when the website's processing changes. The last-updated date appears at the top of the page. Any future non-essential tracking will require a separate assessment, suitable information, and consent where required before it is activated.
