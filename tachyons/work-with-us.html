Junior Electrical Engineer01

Job Overview

We are seeking a hands-on and motivated Junior Electrical Engineer to join our technical team. In this role, you will be involved in practical electrical assembly, prototyping, and testing activities. This is an ideal opportunity for a recent graduate or a student in a technical/industrial field looking to apply their skills in a dynamic work environment.

Key Responsibilities

  • Assemble, wire, and build custom electrical components, including custom battery packs.
  • Perform high-quality hand soldering, prototyping, and hardware rework.
  • Read, interpret, and troubleshoot electrical schematics and circuit diagrams.
  • Assist the engineering team with testing, verification, and hardware debugging.
  • Document assembly procedures and technical findings.

Required Qualifications

  • Technical Background: Currently enrolled in or completed a degree/diploma in Electrical Engineering, Industrial Technology, or a related technical field.
  • Hands-on Skills: Solid foundational knowledge of electrical circuits, practical soldering skills, and experience with basic battery pack assembly/wiring.
  • Language Proficiency: Good proficiency in English (written and verbal), sufficient to read technical documentation and collaborate effectively.
  • Strong attention to detail, problem-solving mindset, and practical approach to technical tasks.

Preferred / Nice-to-Have Qualifications

  • Familiarity with PCB design tools such as KiCad (or similar software) for viewing and reviewing schematic layouts.
  • A portfolio or examples of past personal, academic, or hobbyist projects (e.g., DIY electronics, robotics, custom circuitry).

  • Self-motivated, organized, and capable of working independently on content schedules.

Preferred / Nice-to-Have Qualifications

  • A personal portfolio or case studies of previous social media accounts, campaigns, or creative digital projects managed.

Embedded Software Engineer03

Job Overview

We are seeking a driven and analytically minded Embedded Software Engineer to join our team. In this position, you will develop, test, and maintain code for hardware integration and microcontroller-based systems. We value solid foundational engineering, genuine problem-solving capabilities, and the ability to design robust solutions from first principles.

Key Responsibilities

  • Write, debug, and optimize code for microcontroller architectures and hardware peripherals.
  • Develop backend logic, automation scripts, and hardware interfaces primarily using Python.
  • Integrate software routines with sensors, actuators, and communication protocols (e.g., UART, I2C, SPI).
  • Conduct hands-on hardware-in-the-loop debugging and systematic troubleshooting.
  • Maintain clean, well-documented, and version-controlled codebases.

Required Qualifications

  • Educational Background: Currently enrolled in or completed a technical/industrial diploma or degree (Computer Science, Electronics, Telecommunications, or related fields).
  • Microcontroller Expertise: In-depth, practical knowledge of microcontroller programming, register-level configuration, and hardware abstraction.
  • Core Language Proficiency: Strong hands-on proficiency in Python for application logic, scripting, or interfacing.
  • Independent Problem Solving: Demonstrated ability to reason through architectural logic, debug code, and write structured algorithms independently, without reliance on AI code-generation tools.
  • Strong algorithmic foundation and systematic approach to error diagnosis.

Preferred / Nice-to-Have Qualifications

  • Familiarity with C or C++ in embedded environments.
  • Practical exposure to version control systems (Git) and command-line environments.
  • Past personal or academic projects involving robotics, automation, or custom electronics.

CAD & Rapid Prototyping Specialist04

Job Overview

We are looking for a creative and technically skilled CAD & Rapid Prototyping Specialist to lead our digital modeling and physical fabrication efforts. In this role, you will take mechanical concepts from 3D models to working prototypes using in-house fabrication machinery, while collaborating closely with the electronics team on enclosure and PCB integration.

Key Responsibilities

  • Design 3D mechanical models, assemblies, and functional parts primarily utilizing Autodesk Fusion 360.
  • Inspect, interface, and align enclosures with PCB designs using KiCad to ensure seamless mechanical-electrical packaging.
  • Operate, maintain, and slice files for in-house 3D printers (FDM/SLA) and CNC/laser cutters.
  • Rapidly iterate on prototypes, diagnosing tolerance, clearance, and material constraints.
  • Maintain clean design files, version histories, and manufacturing-ready exports (STEP, DXF, STL).

Required Qualifications

  • Core 3D CAD Proficiency: Advanced working knowledge of Autodesk Fusion 360 (parametric modeling, sheet metal/assemblies).
  • Electronics Familiarity: Practical experience with KiCad for reading PCB layouts and coordinating mechanical fitment.
  • Hands-on Prototyping: Direct, practical experience setting up and running 3D printers and laser cutting systems (calibrations, slicing settings, material selection).
  • Portfolio Required: A portfolio or project log demonstrating tangible past designs, 3D models, and physical fabrication projects.

Preferred Qualifications & Culture Fit

  • Great social energy, a relaxed attitude, and appreciation for unwinding with a cold beer after a productive build session.
  • Strong curiosity for maker culture, robotics, and hands-on hardware hacking.
